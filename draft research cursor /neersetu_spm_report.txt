1. Executive Summary
------------------------------------------------------------
This report documents complete software project management planning and execution for NeerSetu. The project objective is to build an integrated platform that ingests water quality and public health proxy data, predicts short-term risk, and provides actionable alerts for district administrators.

The report covers initiation, planning, scheduling, estimation, risk handling, quality control, release strategy, and maintenance governance. Unlike a research paper, this document emphasizes management decisions, process discipline, stakeholder coordination, and delivery evidence.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. Project Charter
------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Vision
Enable district teams to detect likely water-borne disease escalation early enough to act before full outbreak spread.

2.2 Mission
Deliver a modular, explainable, and operationally practical surveillance software platform with dashboard and API integrations.

2.3 Business Need
- Fragmented manual workflows delay intervention.
- Existing systems lack predictive prioritization.
- Alert decisions are often undocumented and inconsistent.

2.4 Success Criteria
- Working end-to-end data pipeline.
- Reliable model inference API.
- Tiered alert dashboard with explanation panels.
- Pilot-ready documentation and SOP package.

2.5 Constraints
- Limited labeled data quality in early phase.
- Tight academic timeline.
- Multi-component stack requiring coordination.

2.6 Assumptions
- Domain experts available for threshold validation.
- Synthetic dataset can support initial model engineering.
- Deployment environment available for pilot demonstration.

11. Test Strategy and Results Summary
------------------------------------------------------------
11.1 Test Levels
- Unit Tests: data transforms, threshold logic.
- Integration Tests: service communication and error handling.
- System Tests: end-to-end alert lifecycle.
- UAT: analyst workflow simulation.

11.2 Defect Classification
- Critical: breaks risk scoring or alert integrity.
- Major: affects usability or reliability significantly.
- Minor: cosmetic or non-blocking behavior issues.

11.3 Exit Criteria
- 0 open critical defects,
- acceptable major defect threshold,
- agreed UAT scenarios passed.

12. Risk Management
------------------------------------------------------------
12.1 Risk Register (Sample)
R1 Data drift causes model quality drop.
Mitigation: scheduled monitoring and retraining trigger.

R2 Late reporting reduces forecast reliability.
Mitigation: completeness scoring and confidence annotation.

R3 Integration failures across services.
Mitigation: contract tests and staged rollout.

R4 Alert fatigue due to false positives.
Mitigation: threshold tuning and tiered policy.

R5 Team bandwidth constraints.
Mitigation: backlog prioritization and scope freeze windows.

12.2 Risk Review Cadence
Biweekly risk board with severity updates and action owners.

------------------------------------------------------------

- Calibration is as important as raw model accuracy.
- Stakeholder language matters: explanations improved adoption.
